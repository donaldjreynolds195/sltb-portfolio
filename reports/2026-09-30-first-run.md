live status codes come from the first GitHub Actions run. What we could confirm

**Takeaway:** the NIH site restructured `/about-nih/who-we-are/` and
`/about-nih/what-we-do/` into `/about-nih/organization/`. That one change
touches 8 of the book's 22 content pages.

## Leadership drift

See [leadership-drift.md](leadership-drift.md). **22 of 33** named positions
(67%) now have a different person, including the NIH Director, four of five
Deputy Directors, and 17 of 27 Institute and Center directors. 14 of the
current office holders are acting.

## Volumes 2, 3 and DAB Essentials

- **Typo in Vol 2.** The "About NIA" pages (PDF pages 4-6) cite
  `https://www.nih.nih.gov/about`. The intended source is `https://www.nia.nih.gov/about`.
  The first live check will show whether that host resolves or redirects.
- **Vol 3 barely cites its sources.** Only 2 of its 17 content pages carry a
  footer URL. Everything else (staff, meetings, funding tables) was internal
  or compiled, so there's no public page to check it against.
- **DAB Essentials is the most at-risk volume.** It is the only one with live
  links, and most of them go to individual director bio pages: 15 have a
  person's name in the URL (for example `nei.nih.gov/.../michael-f-chiang-md`).
  When a director leaves, those pages are usually retired or redirected.
  It also links to FlipHTML5 copies of the other volumes (`online.fliphtml5.com/dsred/...`),
  which the monthly check will cover as well.

## NIA and DAB leadership

See [leadership-drift-nia.md](leadership-drift-nia.md). The NIA Director and
Deputy Director are unchanged. The Division of Aging Biology has a new director
(the book lists an acting director), and NIA opened a search for a permanent
Division of Neuroscience director in July 2026. Seven other NIA roles could not
be verified because nia.nih.gov refuses many automated requests; they are left
as UNKNOWN in the report rather than guessed.
